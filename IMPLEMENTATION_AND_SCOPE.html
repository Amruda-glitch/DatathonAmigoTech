1. Executive Summary

KSP Drishti AI (ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ರಾಜ್ಯ ಪೊಲೀಸ್ - ಕೆ.ಎಸ್.ಪಿ ದೃಷ್ಟಿ ಎಐ) is an enterprise decision support and intelligence web platform engineered for law enforcement agencies, crime analysts, police superintendents, and policymakers in Karnataka.

Integrated directly with ICJS 2.0 (Inter-operable Criminal Justice System) and MCCTNS (Modernized Crime and Criminal Tracking Network & Systems), the platform indexes over 1.42 Million FIR records (2018–2026) to deliver real-time RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) query matching, criminal network visualization, spatial hotspot analytics, automated BNS section mapping, CCTV video analytics, and strict audit logging compliant with the DPDP (Digital Personal Data Protection) Act 2023.

2. What Has Been Implemented

🤖 AI Copilot & Conversational RAG (src/components/CopilotModule.jsx)

  • Natural Language Modus Operandi (MO) Search across 1.42M indexed FIRs.
  • Preset investigation query triggers (Indiranagar Pulsar Chain Snatching, Whitefield Cyber Fraud Syndicate, Mysuru Dasara Burglary Risk, Offender Profile Ramesh @ Bullet).
  • Automated legal section recommendation under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) with confidence scores.
  • SQL query provenance display, FAISS/Cosine similarity metrics, and cryptographic CCTNS SHA-256 evidence hashes.
  • Actionable tactical lead generation (IMEI tower dump subpoenas, pawn shop checks, court bail verifications).

🎥 CCTV Smart Feed & ANPR Monitoring (src/components/CCTVMonitoringModule.jsx)

  • Multi-camera live stream monitoring across strategic intersections (MG Road, Silk Board, Kempegowda Bus Station, Outer Ring Road).
  • ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) & facial recognition alert tracking.
  • Bandwidth, uptime, active stream, and AI inference latency (14ms) analytics.

🕸️ Crime Network Knowledge Graph (src/components/NetworkGraphModule.jsx)

  • Interactive Neo4j graph visualizer displaying node links between habitual suspects, FIRs, vehicles, and financial mule bank accounts.
  • Dynamic node filtering with embedded risk metrics.

🗺️ GIS Map & Spatial Hotspot Analytics (src/components/GISMapModule.jsx)

  • Interactive Leaflet spatial mapping across police divisions (Indiranagar, Jayanagar, Whitefield, Mysuru, Hubballi).
  • Hotspot heatmaps, crime density scores, and festival surge risk forecasting.

👤 Offender Profiling & Dossiers (src/components/OffenderProfileModule.jsx)

  • Habitual offender intelligence dossiers tracking risk scores, MO history, gang associates, and active bail conditions at 1st ACMM Court.

📁 Investigator Workspace (src/components/InvestigatorWorkspaceModule.jsx)

  • Digital case binder management for organizing witness statements, physical evidence SHA-256 hashes, and exporting court briefs.

🔒 AI Governance & DPDP Audit (src/components/AuditGovernanceModule.jsx)

  • DPDP Act 2023 compliance dashboard logging query history, prompt hashes, role access levels, and PII anonymization statistics.

3. Operational & Legal Scopes

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Matrix

Role ID Title / Rank Access Level Permissions & Scope
INVESTIGATOR Sub-Inspector / Inspector Level 1 Local station FIR search, suspect interrogation notes, active case binder, PII masked
ANALYST Crime Analyst Level 2 Cross-station network graphs, MO pattern matching, trend analytics, PII masked
SUPERVISOR Superintendent of Police (SP) Level 3 District-wide GIS heatmaps, squad deployment, bail violation alerts, unmasked PII
POLICYMAKER Joint Secretary (Home Dept) Level 4 State-level crime metrics, policy recommendations, DPDP audit overview

Data Protection & Legal Scope

  • DPDP Act 2023 Compliance: Automatic masking of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like Aadhaar numbers and exact residential addresses for Level 1 & Level 2 users.
  • BNS Legal Mapping: Transitioned from IPC to Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) clauses.
  • Cryptographic Hash Integrity: Immutable SHA-256 evidence logging.
