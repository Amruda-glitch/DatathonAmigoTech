🚓 KSP दृष्टि AI (KSP Drishti AI)

Intelligent Conversational AI & Crime Analytics Platform for Karnataka State Police

React 19.2 Vite 8.1 Tailwind v4 Leaflet GIS ICJS 2.0 / MCCTNS DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

1. Executive Summary

KSP Drishti AI (ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ರಾಜ್ಯ ಪೊಲೀಸ್ - ಕೆ.ಎಸ್.ಪಿ ದೃಷ್ಟಿ ಎಐ) is an enterprise decision support and intelligence web platform engineered for law enforcement agencies, crime analysts, police superintendents, and policymakers across Karnataka State.

Integrated directly with ICJS 2.0 (Inter-operable Criminal Justice System) and MCCTNS (Modernized Crime and Criminal Tracking Network & Systems) repositories, KSP Drishti AI indexes over 1.42 Million FIR records (2018–2026) to provide real-time RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) query matching for Modus Operandi (MO), automated legal section mapping under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), graph-based suspect network link visualization, spatial hotspot analytics, and strict audit logging compliant with the DPDP (Digital Personal Data Protection) Act 2023.

2. Core Modules & Capabilities

🤖 AI Copilot & Conversational RAG (src/components/CopilotModule.jsx)

  • Natural Language MO Search: Query crime patterns, modus operandi, suspect descriptions, and vehicle signatures across millions of FIRs.
  • BNS Legal Mapping: Automated legal mapping to Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) clauses (e.g., BNS Sec 304 Snatching, Sec 310(2) Dacoity) with confidence scores.
  • Auto SQL & Vector Provenance: Full transparency via generated SQL queries and vector retrieval stats with cryptographic SHA-256 CCTNS hashes.
  • Actionable Tactical Leads: AI-recommended investigative next steps (IMEI tower dumps, pawn shop checks, court bail condition verifications).

🎥 CCTV Smart Feed & ANPR Monitoring (src/components/CCTVMonitoringModule.jsx)

  • Live Feed Monitoring: Real-time feeds across high-traffic intersections (MG Road, Silk Board, Kempegowda Bus Station).
  • ANPR & Facial Recognition Alerts: Automated alerts for suspect vehicles and face matches.

🕸️ Crime Network Graph Visualizer (src/components/NetworkGraphModule.jsx)

  • Neo4j Graph Analytics: Interactive visual network mapping relationships between habitual suspects, crime syndicates, linked FIRs, and financial mule bank accounts.

🗺️ GIS Map & Spatial Hotspot Analytics (src/components/GISMapModule.jsx)

  • Interactive Leaflet Mapping: Spatial crime clustering across Karnataka police divisions with temporal surge predictions.

👤 Offender Profiling & Dossiers (src/components/OffenderProfileModule.jsx)

  • Habitual Offender Intelligence: Deep dossiers tracking criminological risk scores, MO history, gang affiliations, and active bail conditions.

📁 Investigator Workspace (src/components/InvestigatorWorkspaceModule.jsx)

  • Digital Case Binder: Interface for Sub-Inspectors and Inspectors to assemble FIR details, witness statements, and evidence hashes.

🔒 Audit & DPDP Governance (src/components/AuditGovernanceModule.jsx)

  • Compliance Dashboard: Immutable logging of all user queries, prompt history, data access levels, and role escalations in compliance with DPDP Act 2023.

3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Role ID Title / Rank Access Level Permissions & View Scopes
INVESTIGATOR Sub-Inspector / Inspector Level 1 Local station FIR search, suspect interrogation notes, active case binder
ANALYST Crime Analyst Level 2 Cross-station network graphs, MO pattern matching, trend analytics
SUPERVISOR Superintendent of Police (SP) Level 3 District-wide GIS heatmaps, squad deployment, bail violation alerts
POLICYMAKER Joint Secretary (Home Dept) Level 4 State-level crime metrics, policy recommendations, DPDP audit overview

6. Security & Governance (DPDP Act 2023)

  • Data Masking: PII (Personally Identifiable Information) such as Aadhaar numbers and residential addresses are masked for access levels below Level 3.
  • Cryptographic Provenance: Every AI response carries a SHA-256 evidence hash timestamped against ICJS 2.0.
  • Audit Lineage: Immutable query logging conforming to Indian Cyber Law standards.
